The Operational Challenge in Security Operations (SOC)

KCG College of Technology • CSE (Cyber Security)
The Enterprise Alert Paradigm
  • Surge in Remote Authentication Breaches: Credential-stuffing and brute-force campaigns (MITRE ATT&CK T1110) represent over 44% of modern unauthorized access attempts.
  • Severe Analyst Fatigue: Tier-1 SOC analysts face thousands of disconnected telemetry events daily, resulting in desensitization and prolonged response latency.
  • Manual Triage Bottlenecks: Traditional log review and incident corroboration consumes an average of 20 to 45 minutes per incident ticket.
The Volatile Forensics Dilemma
  • Evaporating In-Memory Artifacts: Malicious processes, volatile TCP sockets, and active user tokens are lost upon reboot or attacker anti-forensic clearing.
  • Triage Delay Equals Evidence Loss: When forensics is invoked hours after the alert, volatile traces are overwritten by normal OS scheduling.
  • Chain of Custody Vulnerability: Ad-hoc artifact collection without cryptographic binding fails technical admissibility under ISO/IEC 27037 standards.

Limitations & Discussion Slide 09 / 11

Technical Constraints & Observability Considerations

KCG College of Technology • CSE (Cyber Security)
1. Operating System Privilege Boundaries
Querying deep administrative log channels (e.g. raw security event queries via `wevtutil`) requires elevated user tokens. When executed under standard service credentials, the system adheres to honest observability by recording UNAVAILABLE rather than fabricating synthetic telemetry.
2. Host Execution Scope & Multi-Tenancy
The current acquisition engine operates directly on the host running the Python runtime. Multi-subnet enterprise deployments will require remote transport tunneling (e.g. SSH or Wazuh Active Response push channels) to gather forensics from remote endpoints.
3. Volatile Storage Scaling
Iterative attack bursts generate dozens of artifact text files. In enterprise production, a cold S3-compatible object store with immutable retention policies (WORM storage) is recommended to prevent local disk exhaustion.
